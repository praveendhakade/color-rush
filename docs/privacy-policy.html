Color Rush Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Color Rush, an Android game published under the package name com.alphaprogaming24.colorrush.

Information handled by the app

Color Rush stores the following game information only on your device:

  • best score;
  • number of games played;
  • highest combo;
  • sound preference; and
  • haptics preference.

Collection, use, and sharing

Color Rush does not collect or transmit this information to the developer. The app has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, backend service, online leaderboard, or in-app purchases. We do not sell or share your personal information.

Data retention and deletion

Local game information remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Because Color Rush does not receive or store this information on a server, there is no server-side account or game data to delete.

Security

Game information is stored using your device's application storage. No game information is transmitted by Color Rush to the developer.

Changes to this policy

If Color Rush adds features that collect, use, or share data, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Color Rush developer at alphaprogaming24@gmail.com.
